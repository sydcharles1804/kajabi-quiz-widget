**You Have Already Done the Math**

It’s true that full-ride scholarships are hard to come by. And even with great grades, your child still might not get enough money from their college to make the cost “affordable” for your family.

So in the back of your mind, you are already asking yourself if you can work more hours, tap home equity, or touch your retirement to pay the balance.

The question is not whether you love your child enough to do it. Of course you do. The question is what that bill could cost the rest of your life.

Delayed retirement?

Years of private student loan repayments?

Telling your child you just can’t afford their dream school – after they worked so hard, all these years?

If you’re grappling with these questions, SCHOLAR Prep™ was designed for you.

**High Income Closes Some Doors. But It Doesn’t Lock You Out.**

Need-based aid and academic merit are only two parts of the college-money picture.

After years of working with families like yours, I’ve identified 14 Scholarship Categories.

There is money connected to service, activities, special interests, careers, employers, professional associations, civic organizations, colleges, and more. And, there are opportunities right under your nose that you don’t know to ask about.

THE REFRAME: The goal is not to chase every scholarship on the internet. Instead, we’ll strengthen your child’s SCHOLAR Profile so they can attract opportunities that fit who they are and where they are going.

**SCHOLAR Prep™ Is for the Mom Who…**

Earns too much to count on meaningful need-based aid — but not enough to treat six-figure college costs like pocket change.

**I Know What It Means to Be “Too Rich” for Aid**

I did not qualify for federal or state grants when I went to college either. I learned that scholarship success could not be reduced to finding a few national competitions and hoping for the best. I eventually found the money that fit me, though – and went to grad school for FREE.

That lesson became the foundation of my work.

For more than a decade, I have helped students earn well over $12 million in scholarships and gain admission to colleges including Yale, UPenn, Cornell, Pomona, Howard, Morehouse, Emory, Bucknell, and other highly selective schools.

My methods have helped a student receive a Morehouse full-ride scholarship plus a $10,000 study-abroad stipend, coming from a multiple six-figure household.

I have helped a family (dad an engineer & mom a nurse) secure an additional $64,000 in aid by writing a solid financial aid appeal letter.

And most of our students attend college for less than $1,000 out of pocket per year.

Those results did not come because of their parents’ income. Or grades alone. They came from understanding the student, the money, the fit, and the strategy.
