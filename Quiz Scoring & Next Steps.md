# Quiz Scoring & Next Steps

Content spec for the SCHOLAR Score quiz results, by grade band × score tier. Each tier has two segments:

- **Segment One — Immediate Quiz Result**: the on-screen "snippet" shown right after the quiz.
- **Segment Two — Email: Next Steps**: appended after Segment One and sent as the full report email.

> **Editorial note:** Keep Segment One relatively "clean." Its job is recognition, emotional relevance, and anticipation — not the full sale. Segment Two can then provide genuine help while demonstrating the difference between free information and the paid offer:
>
> *"The free result identifies the position. [SCHOLAR Prep / Scholars Get Dollars] provides the precision."*
